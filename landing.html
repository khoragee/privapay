Traditional onchain payroll is impossible — every salary, every transfer, every amount is permanently public. PrivaPay changes that. We use FHE to encrypt salary values before they ever touch the blockchain, so the numbers you pay stay between you and your team.

01

Encrypted Salaries

All salary values are stored as FHE ciphertexts (euint64) on-chain. The encrypted value is mathematically indistinguishable from random noise to anyone without the key.

02

Per-Employee ACL

Zama's Access Control List ensures only the employer and the relevant employee can decrypt their salary. Access is enforced cryptographically, not by policy.

03

Payroll Periods

Employees claim once per period. Employers advance the period to open the next cycle. Clean, auditable, and entirely onchain.

04

HR Band Checks

HR admins can verify whether a salary falls within a compliance band — without seeing the exact amount. The result is an encrypted boolean, decryptable only by HR.

05

Encrypted Totals

Total payroll disbursed is tracked as an encrypted accumulator. Employers can request a public decryption proof for auditing without exposing individual salaries.

06

Emergency Controls

Pause and unpause payroll instantly. Batch salary setting for entire teams in one transaction. Designed for enterprise operational needs.

How it works
under the hood.

STEP 01

Employer encrypts salary

Salary amount is encrypted client-side using the Zama Relayer SDK. A Zero-Knowledge Proof of Knowledge is generated alongside the ciphertext.

input.add64(salaryAmount)
const enc = await input.encrypt()
// → handle + ZKPoK proof
STEP 02

Contract validates & stores

The contract calls FHE.fromExternal() to validate the ZKPoK before storing the ciphertext. ACL is set — only employer + employee can access.

euint64 salary = FHE.fromExternal(
encSalary, inputProof
)
FHE.allow(salary, employee)
STEP 03

Employee claims salary

The employee calls claimSalary(). The encrypted amount is added to the accumulator. No plaintext amount is ever stored or emitted.

_totalDisbursed = FHE.add(
_totalDisbursed,
_salary[employee]
)
STEP 04

Employee decrypts privately

The employee uses the Relayer SDK to re-encrypt their balance under their personal key. The plaintext never leaves their device.

const salary = await instance
.reencrypt(handle, privateKey,
publicKey, sig, contract, addr)

Why FHE and
not ZK or MPC?

Zero-knowledge proofs can prove a fact without revealing data — but they can't compute on persistent hidden state. MPC distributes trust but requires multiple online parties. FHE is the only approach that lets a single smart contract compute arbitrary functions on encrypted state that persists across transactions.
